Item 3: Beaumont-Tully Rural Literacy Fund

Marisol reported that program officer Celeste Harrow at the Beaumont-Tully Rural Literacy Fund invited us to present Route Eighteen at their December 4 review. We will ask for $85,000 toward the $148,000 total. The county capital award of $40,000 is committed, and the spring donor campaign covers the last $23,000.

June asked for a short slide deck to go with the presentation. Marisol will own it. Draft to the board by November 20, 2026, final to Celeste by November 27.

Instructions the board agreed on for the deck:

1. Keep it to eight slides or fewer. Ms. Harrow said reviewers get ten minutes per applicant.
2. Open with the problem: 11 missed stops last year, all from the 2009 vehicle, and four north-county schools with 310 students waiting to join.
3. Show the full $148,000 budget line by line and all three funding sources, so the Fund sees that $85,000 is not the whole cost.
4. Abner insisted: the third-grade reading result (41 to 49 percent at six schools) goes in only with its caveat. Say plainly that the state tutors were in four of those schools and that we do not claim the gain. Do not round it into "raised reading scores."
5. Lila: no photos of children's faces and no student names. Ms. Akin's quote is fine because she gave permission.

Pete will send updated stop counts if anything changes before November 20.

Next meeting: November 18, 2026.

Item 3: Reade Fund pitch

Teo reported that Ruth Ellingsen, program officer at the Tamsin Reade Fund for Rural Arts, invited us to send a short deck before their December committee meeting. She asked specifically about the eastern county touring gap.

Owen asked for a deck that requests $45,000 from the Reade Fund to close the gap on the $68,500 Eastern County touring company. Priya confirmed the $23,500 already in hand: $12,000 projected touring fees and $11,500 from the County Arts Council rural access fund.

Deadline: draft to Owen and Dela by November 13. Final goes to Ruth Ellingsen by November 20, 2026.

Instructions agreed for the deck:

1. Lead with the eastern county schools, not with our history. The 11 requests we turned down and the four of five schools with no arts teacher beyond music should be up front.

3. Keep the caveat on the confidence survey (41 to 74 percent). Hannah: "If we drop the caveat, Ruth will find it in the report and we lose her trust." Do not present it as proof we caused the change.
4. No student photos and no last names. Wren's quote may be used with first name and age only; consent is on file.
5. Eight slides or fewer. The ask gets its own slide with the full $68,500 budget and the $45,000 gap stated plainly.

Dela will send Teo the report file and brand sheet this week.

Action: Teo owns the draft.

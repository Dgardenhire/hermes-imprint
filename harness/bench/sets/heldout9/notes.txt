Breckwater Valley Robotics Club - Board Meeting Notes
Tuesday, November 3, 2026, 6:30 p.m., Mill Road shop

Present: Ruth Ellingboe (Board Chair), Glenn Asheford (Treasurer), Tomas Vrba (Program Coordinator), Priya Lindqvist (Coach), Dale Kucharski (Board Member)

1. Wickline Rural Futures Fund

Tomas reported that Hana Ostberg, program officer at the Wickline Rural Futures Fund, invited us to present on December 9. She asked for a short deck in advance. Ruth moved that we ask Wickline for $37,400 to fund the full FY2027 expansion for one year: second van ($9,800), third coach stipend ($12,000), kits for three new teams ($11,400), and added travel ($4,200). Glenn confirmed the figures match the FY2026 annual report and that the $148,600 base budget is covered by our five current sources. Motion carried 5-0.

Deadline: Tomas will send the final deck to Hana by Friday, December 4, 2026. Board review copy due to Ruth by November 24.

2. Instructions for the deck (agreed by the board)

- Keep it to eight slides or fewer. Hana said she reads decks on her phone.
- Lead with the 52 students who finished the season and the expansion to Ostrander, not the state championship placing.
- State the 14-of-18 postsecondary result with its caveat in plain words: self-reported, small group, no comparison group. Dale asked that we not imply the club caused it.
- Show the $37,400 as the four line items, and make clear Wickline money pays only for the expansion, not existing operations.
- No student photos without a signed release on file, and no student last names. Priya will check releases.

3. Other business

Glenn reported the reserve at $14,200. Meeting adjourned 7:52 p.m.

Haverlin Creek Paddle Works
Board meeting notes, October 21, 2026
Present: Dev Okonkwo-Hale (board chair), Mara Vellacott (executive director), Teodor Brisk (treasurer), Lena Farrow-Quist (board member, fundraising committee)

Item 3: Ardena Lake Community Foundation proposal

Lena reported that the Ardena Lake Community Foundation has invited us to present at its December 11 grants committee meeting. The foundation's youth recreation fund makes grants up to $50,000.

Mara proposed we ask for $40,000 toward the $58,000 first-year cost of Weekday Water. The other $18,000 is the increase we requested from the Tansey County Recreation District. Teodor confirmed the numbers match the FY2026 report: $186,400 in expenses, $191,000 in revenue, and the $45,000 Hollins Family Foundation grant ending this year.

Dev asked Mara to prepare a short slide deck for the committee. Final draft is due to Dev and Lena by Friday, December 4, so the board can review it before it goes to the foundation on December 8.

Instructions for the deck, agreed by the board:

1. Keep it to eight slides or fewer. The committee gives each applicant ten minutes.
2. Put the $40,000 ask on the first or second slide, not at the end.
3. Show the 84 percent assessment result with the caveat about the 35 paddlers who did not take the final assessment and the change in assessors. Do not present it as a clean before-and-after.
4. Do not say or imply the Recreation District's $18,000 is confirmed. Say it is pending the January budget vote.
5. Do not use photos in which any child can be identified. Use boats, water and the boathouse only.

Action: Mara to draft the deck. Lena to send Mara the foundation's committee roster and guidelines by October 28.

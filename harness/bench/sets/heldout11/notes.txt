Bramble Creek Mobile Market
Staff and board officers meeting, November 6, 2026
Present: Lena Ostrowe (executive director), Marcus Villanueva (board chair), Ruth Akande (treasurer), Cal Brightwater (market manager)

Item 1: Ostrander Rural Health Trust

Lena reported that the program officer at the Ostrander Rural Health Trust, Gwen Halvorsen, invited us to send a short deck before their winter docket. Their docket closes Friday, December 4, 2026. Lena will draft; Marcus and Ruth review by Monday, November 30.

The ask is $95,000 toward the $145,000 first-year cost of the eastern route. Ruth confirmed the rest is covered: $30,000 committed by the Hollins County Health District and $20,000 from the spring donor campaign.

Marcus said Ostrander funds rural health access, not food programs as such, so the deck has to connect the route to health.

Instructions for the deck, agreed by everyone present:

1. Keep it to 8 slides or fewer. Gwen said she reads these on her phone.
2. Put the ask, $95,000 for the eastern route, on the first or second slide, along with the full $145,000 budget broken into its five lines.
3. Use the produce prescription result (A1C down 0.6 points among 74 completers), but state the caveat in plain words on the same slide: no comparison group, and the 19 dropouts are not counted. Do not call it proof.
4. Do not claim we end or solve hunger, and do not use county-wide food insecurity statistics we did not measure ourselves.
5. Use Doreen's quote with only "Doreen M." No customer photos and no full names of customers or patients.

Cal will pull a stop map of the five eastern towns for Lena by November 20.

Next meeting: November 30, 2026.

Tallow Creek Respite Cooperative
Board meeting notes, Wednesday, November 4, 2026
Present: Osric Pellam (chair), Juniper Haldane (treasurer), Mirela Aske (executive director), Benedikt Orrin (program director)

Item 3: Ostrander Valley Health Fund

Mirela reported that Teodora Vance, program officer at the Ostrander Valley Health Fund, invited us to submit a short deck for their winter caregiving round after reading our FY2026 report. Ask: $120,000 for one year toward the FY2027 overnight respite pilot and the two eastern township workers. Total expansion cost is $165,000; the county Area Agency on Aging has already committed $45,000.

Deadline: deck to Teodora by Tuesday, December 1, 2026. Mirela wants a draft to the board by Friday, November 20.

Juniper noted that Fenwright's $150,000 does not renew at the same level after FY2027 and asked that we not hide how much we lean on the county and Fenwright (64 percent). Osric agreed.

Instructions for the deck, agreed by the board:
1. Keep it to eight slides or fewer. Teodora said she reads them on her phone.
2. Lead with the waitlist: 41 days down to 12. That is our strongest number and it is not in dispute.
3. Show the strain result (27.4 to 19.8) only with the caveat in plain view: 96 of 171 families answered, no comparison group, our own staff gave the survey. Do not call the program "evidence-based" and do not say respite keeps people out of nursing homes.
4. Show the full $165,000 by line, with the county's $45,000 visible, so the $120,000 ask is clearly the gap.
5. No photos of caregivers or the people they care for, and no names. The quote can run without attribution beyond "caregiver, Upper Tallow township."

Benedikt will pull the numbers. Mirela owns the draft.

VESSICK NEIGHBORHOOD MEDIATION CENTER
Board meeting notes, Tuesday, October 20, 2026
Present: Ines Dalgarno (chair), Marisol Pyke (treasurer), Ruth Ambersley, Owen Carrick, Teodor Halvik (executive director, staff)

Item 3: Merrow Valley Community Trust

Teodor reported that Hollis Brandt, program officer at the Merrow Valley Community Trust, invited us to submit for the Trust's winter housing round. The Trust wants a short slide deck first, not a full proposal. If the deck lands, they will ask for a budget and a site visit in January.

Ask: $60,000 toward the Tenant Early Resolution Desk. Desk costs $96,000 for its first 12 months; the county court has already committed $36,000. Marisol confirmed the numbers match the FY2026 report.

Deadline: final deck to Hollis by Friday, December 4, 2026. Draft to the board by Friday, November 20.

Discussion and instructions for the deck:

1. Ines: no more than eight slides. Hollis reads on her phone between meetings.
2. Teodor: put the $60,000 ask on slide one or two, not at the end.
3. Ruth: the 85% figure only goes in with its caveat on the same slide. We reached 52 of 61 households, it is self-reported, and there is no comparison group. Do not say or imply that we prevent evictions.
4. Marisol: show all five funding sources with amounts so the Trust sees the court contract is our largest source and that they would not be carrying us.
5. Owen: no participant names or photos. The tenant quote stays anonymous.

Action: Teodor drafts; Ruth reviews the numbers slide before it goes to the board.

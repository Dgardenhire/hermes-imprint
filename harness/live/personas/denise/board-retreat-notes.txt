Board retreat notes, September 13

Present: board chair Lorraine Okafor, treasurer Dev Patel, executive director Marcus Bell, communications director Denise Alvarez, six board members.

The treasurer reported that reserves stand at 3.2 months of operating expenses, below the board's target of 4 months.
The board asked staff to bring a one-page summary of the FY2027 budget request to the October 21 board meeting.
Board members asked for a short deck they can use with funders when they talk about the sixth site.
The board agreed to vote on the FY2027 budget on October 21.

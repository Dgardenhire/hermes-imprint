Cedar Hollow Warming Center: Guest and Volunteer Survey, March 2026
(Sample material for trying Letterpress. Cedar Hollow is a fictional city.)

The Department of Community Services surveyed guests during the last two weeks of the season and volunteers by email after it closed.

Guests
188 guests answered the survey. 91 percent said they felt safe at the center. 74 percent said the walk or ride to the center was the hardest part of getting there. The most requested change was earlier opening hours: the doors open at 7 p.m., and guests asked for 5 p.m. on the coldest nights.

One guest wrote: "Having a place to go when it drops below freezing is the difference between making it through the night and not."

Volunteers
146 volunteers answered. 88 percent said they plan to volunteer again next season. The most common request was more training before the first night, especially on what to do in a medical emergency.

# Cedar Hollow Winter Warming Center: Season Report, 2025-26

Prepared by the Department of Community Services for the City Council. Cedar Hollow is a fictional city; this report is sample material for trying Letterpress.

## What the center did

The warming center at the Maple Street Recreation Hall opened on the night of December 1, 2025 and closed for the season on March 15, 2026. It was open 87 nights, every night the forecast low was 32 degrees or colder, and on 9 additional nights with freezing rain.

Over the season the center recorded 2,914 overnight stays by 412 different guests. The busiest night was January 21, 2026, when 61 guests stayed during a stretch of five nights below 10 degrees. On an average night, 33 guests stayed.

## Who it served

Of the 412 guests, 118 were over the age of 60 and 37 were veterans. Staff connected 64 guests with permanent or transitional housing before the season ended, up from 29 the season before.

## What it cost

The season cost $318,400. The largest costs were overnight staff at $171,900, cots, bedding and laundry at $48,200, and meals at $41,700. The cost per overnight stay was $109.

The city paid $205,000 from the general fund. A county grant covered $80,000, and private donations covered the remaining $33,400.

## Volunteers

Volunteers gave 3,260 hours over the season, mostly in the evening meal and morning shifts. The Cedar Hollow Faith Alliance and two neighborhood associations organized most of the volunteer schedule.

## Recommendations for next season

Staff recommend opening on November 15 instead of December 1, adding a second site on the east side of the city so guests do not have to cross the river at night, and setting the opening trigger at 35 degrees instead of 32. Staff estimate the second site would add $142,000 to the season's cost.
